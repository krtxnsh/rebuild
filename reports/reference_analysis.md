## A. Electricity Room

timber framing (corner posts, horizontal band at door-head height, gable diagonals, thick curved barge boards, overhanging
golden ridge beam); front-facing gable with steep **concave-curved** roof (flared eaves) of large overlapping **slate-blue/grey
shingles** (some missing in ref); **circular gable window** with thick golden ring frame, grid mullion, warm glow; tall brick
**chimney** behind the left roof slope with stepped double flue; wide doorway with arched timber head and **two plank doors
open outward**; interior visible through door: wall panel with round **"VOLTS" meters**/gauges, standing **copper coils**,
brass/copper apparatus, plank floor; **hanging bulbs** strung under the eave band (3 left, 4 right); small golden-framed glowing
window on right wall; wooden **waterwheel** on the right wall near the front with a water chute pouring into a stone-edged
basin; three wooden **utility poles** with crossarms, white ceramic insulators and drooping wires to the house; wrought-iron
fence with ball finials; ladder (left); stacked cable spools; ground lantern; fallen pole with coiled cable.
**Multi-view sheet adds:** lantern on bracket beside the door; rear wall with single plank door and small window; ladder on
rear corner; fence gate.
**Proportions (est.):** footprint ~5.0 x 5.6 m, eave ~3.0 m, ridge ~6.5 m, door opening ~2.0 x 2.5 m, round window ~1.3 m dia,
waterwheel ~2.6 m dia.

- **Electricity Room**: right; gable + doors face camera-left toward the bridge; waterwheel on its right side.
- **Stream**: enters upper centre-right, winds down between water plant and electricity room, passes under the **central arched
